Privacy notice

Effective 16 November 2026

The AI Visibility Intelligence Platform is operated by Abdul Hafeez. For support, privacy questions or data requests, email hafeezpakhton@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process your account email, password hash, account API key, projects, website URLs, scan reports and imported Search Console data. Reports may include public website content and excerpts. Hosting logs may include IP addresses, timestamps, requested routes, status codes and request identifiers. Do not submit confidential content.

How we use it

We use information to authenticate accounts, perform requested scans, save and compare reports, display search performance, provide exports and operate and troubleshoot the service. Essential session cookies keep you signed in.

Google Search Console

Connecting Google is optional. We request read-only Search Console access to list the properties you can access and retrieve the performance data you select. We do not request permission to change your Search Console settings. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted on the server and are not returned to your browser.

Google data is used to provide these user-facing search performance features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. We do not permit human access to Google user data except with your affirmative consent for a specific purpose, as necessary for security, to comply with applicable law, or for internal operations on aggregated and anonymized data where permitted by Google's policy.

The service's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

The app is hosted on Railway and uses MongoDB storage. Google processes authorization and Search Console API requests. A website you scan receives the crawler's requests. Optional PageSpeed requests are processed by Google when that feature is used. Providers process the information needed to deliver these features under their own applicable terms. Password recovery email delivery and production backup storage are still being configured for this beta; we will update this notice when those services are activated.

Retention and deletion

Account data, projects, saved reports and imported search data remain until you remove them using the available controls or request deletion. There is no automatic age-based deletion of these records in the current beta. Operational logs are retained according to the hosting provider's settings; a fixed log-retention period has not yet been established for this beta. Production backups and a backup-retention policy have not yet been configured.

Disconnecting Google removes the locally stored Google credentials and attempts to revoke Google's grant. If revocation cannot be confirmed, remove the connection through your Google Account permissions. Disconnecting does not delete previously imported search data. Clear imports, delete the relevant project or delete your account to remove associated data. Account deletion removes your account and its associated database records. Export information you need before deleting it.

Your choices and requests

Use the account controls to export supported data, disconnect Google or delete your account. Email the contact above to request access, correction or deletion, or ask about rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on an account request. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection; protect your password and API key.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the service's data practices change. Contact us if a change affects your use of the service.
